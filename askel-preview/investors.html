Why Askel

Built-in edge. From day one.

Most micro-PE funds rely on spreadsheets and gut feel. We've built proprietary infrastructure that compounds our deal velocity and diligence quality over time.

01

50-Item Due Diligence

Every target passes through a rigorous structured DD framework — cash flow durability, customer concentration, equipment condition, staffing risk, and more. Nothing gets waved through.

02

Askel OS

Our proprietary deal management system tracks every target across sourcing, screening, IC, DD, and onboarding. Full pipeline visibility — no spreadsheets, no blind spots.

03

Steve — Proprietary AI

Our in-house AI analyst runs weekly sourcing sweeps across broker networks, flags weak signals, scores targets against thesis criteria, and maintains the knowledge base. Human judgment closes; AI never sleeps.

04

Broker Network

100+ broker partners across Finland and Scandinavia give us access to 400+ companies in active deal flow. Many of our best leads arrive before they're ever listed publicly.
