Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Askel Ventures Oy respects your privacy. This page explains what personal data we collect through askelventures.com, why, and what rights you have over it.

1. Who we are

This website (askelventures.com) is operated by Askel Ventures Oy ("Askel", "we", "us"), Business ID 3340587-2, registered in Finland.

For any question about this policy or your personal data, contact us at hello@askelventures.com.

2. What data we collect

This site does not have user accounts, and it does not run tracking or analytics scripts.

We collect personal data only when you give it to us directly — for example, by emailing hello@askelventures.com, or by booking a meeting through the external Google Calendar link on our Contact section. In those cases we receive whatever you share: typically your name, email address, and the content of your message or meeting request.

Our hosting and security provider (Cloudflare) may process standard technical data such as IP address and request logs, purely to keep the site running securely. We do not have access to this beyond aggregate, non-identifying traffic information.

3. How we use your data

We use the data you share with us to respond to your inquiry, schedule meetings, and — where relevant — to evaluate a potential business relationship (for example, if you're a founder exploring a sale, or an investor interested in our fund).

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Third-party services

Booking a meeting through our Contact section takes you to Google Calendar, a third-party service governed by Google's own privacy policy. We don't control how Google handles that data.

Links to portfolio companies (such as melers.fi and turunsuurpesula.fi) and to other external sites are provided for convenience; those sites have their own privacy practices.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected — generally, for the duration of an active conversation or relationship, plus a reasonable period afterward for our own records, unless a longer period is required by law (for example, accounting or contractual obligations).

6. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to request that your data be provided to you in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@askelventures.com. If you believe we haven't handled your data properly, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman in Finland (tietosuoja.fi).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our website or practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
