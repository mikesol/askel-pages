II The Engine — Case Study

Proof-of-concept: Melers Oy.

Industrial laundry for elderly care and hotels, plus consumer dry cleaning — Turku region, operating since 1967. Acquired 100%, closed January 2026, in under 100 days.

Business
Revenue ~€490K, EBITDA €115K (23% margin) · 400 customers · acquired at ~3.5x EBITDA
Financing
€400K price — 55% bank loan (OP, Finnvera-backed), 25% seller financing, 20% founder equity. No outside capital.
Value creation
New branding, AI-enabled ERP, standardized sales & marketing, new outbound system, growth operator — targeting +10% YoY EBITDA growth.
