H3 — Non-listed weak signals
new pipeline shipped
PRH open data → finder.fi enrichment · weekly, Wednesday · cron-weak-signal-sweep
What changed

This is where nearly all of the month's build effort went. H3 moved from "the sole audience for autonomous outreach, run manually" to a real automated pipeline:

prospect-discovery (2026-08-28) — PRH industry-code + town + age filtering. Found that the TOIMI3 classification is retired (every company under it is dissolved) and avoided that trap; all 12 thesis sector codes verified live under TOIMI4.

enrich-contacts (2026-08-28) — scrapes company websites for contact info (Brave → own domain → contact page), handling @-obfuscation and JS-cloaked addresses. Email hit rate went from 4/10 to 7/10. Also stopped misreading e-invoicing operator domains (Netvisor, Maventa, Apix) as "already acquired by a group" signals, while keeping genuine acquisition signals (e.g. a target now sending mail from an acquirer's domain).

Outreach channel rule codified (2026-08-28, knowledge/outreach/autonomy-policy.md) — only H3 leads (companies that were never listed for sale) may receive the automatic Simo cold email. The approved opener only makes sense to someone who hasn't decided to sell yet; leads from H1/H2/H4/H5 are Trello-card-only, never emailed autonomously. Enforced in code, not just policy — the send script refuses anything whose source isn't a non-listed channel. Caps: 8 sends/run, 15/24h, 20s gap, dedup, an approval gate file, and reply-based opt-out wired into cron-email-check.

First run, 2026-09-02 — laundry sector: 96 active companies discovered, 19 enriched, 1 candidate (already had an old Trello card, so no new card) — but it triggered the first fully autonomous cold email the pipeline has ever sent.

Read with caution

One run, one thin result. The 10-sector rotation means each sector only comes up roughly once every ten weeks — worth another rotation or two before judging whether this actually surfaces volume.

H4 — Yrityspörssi email alerts
the month's incident
yritysporssi.fi saved-search alerts · weekdays, 3x/day · cron-yritysporssi-alerts
Root cause, finally found

yritysporssi.fi sits behind AWS WAF with a captcha action keyed on the server's Hetzner IP specifically — 405 from the datacenter IP, 200 from anywhere else. It's an IP-reputation block, not the browser-fingerprint theory the docs carried for months, and steel-browser can't route around it since it shares the box's own egress IP. Sourcing here runs entirely off saved-search email alerts instead.

Three compounding failures, all found and fixed the same day (2026-08-28)

1. The account was never actually live. The 2026-08-03 signup was an unverified stub — nobody could log in for over three weeks.

2. The saved searches were self-sabotaging. All four original alerts carried a €400K–2M asking-price filter, which cut the site's own search from 805 results to 36 — a 95.5% loss — because Finnish listings quote "tarjous" rather than a number, so almost everything got filtered out by accident.

3. Sector scoring drifted from the thesis. The cron was scoring against inferred sector labels instead of playbook-thesis's actual sector list — flagging non-thesis sectors like maanrakennus while missing 31 live auto-repair listings.

A backfill measured the damage before fixing it: of 98 live thesis-sector listings, only 10 had ever reached the email channel — 88 never did. Scoring the backfill found 19 Tier A and 14 Tier B candidates, 28 of 37 owner- (not broker-) listed.

Fix

The four filtered alerts were deleted and replaced with six price-filter-free alerts (IDs 30540–30545) segmented by revenue band, €400K–5M. Result was immediate: the first post-fix sweep recorded 805 available / 61 delivered / 29 candidates / 28 cards in one run — which accounts for almost the entire month's card output.

Residual, undocumented-as-fixed

The email channel still only carries title, location, and revenue band. Profit, age, and asking price never appear in the email, and listing detail pages remain WAF-blocked — so H4 leads still can't be scored pass/fail outright, only flagged for manual verification.

02Cross-cutting: making silent failure loud

Everything above — the dead H4 account, the price filter, the sector drift, the missing H5 subscription — was invisible for weeks or months because nothing was watching the pipeline itself. That changed on 2026-08-28, the same day all five issues surfaced.

cron-sourcing-health
new infra
daily, 08:00 → #admin · posts only on failure

scripts/sourcing-health.py runs five assertions across every sourcing cron: YIELD (are leads converting), EMPTY SOURCE (did a run come back suspiciously empty), COLLAPSE (did volume fall off a cliff), ZERO STREAK (how many runs in a row produced nothing), and SILENT / NEVER RAN (is a cron overdue or has it never fired). Deliberately posts only when something's wrong — an "all clear" every day would just train people to stop reading it. A 30-day funnel view (the numbers in section 00) was also added to the weekly digest.

Three rounds of false positives have hit since launch, each fixed with a recorded reason rather than silently tuned away: a Monday SILENT alarm on the weekday-only H4 cron that didn't account for the weekend gap (fixed by raising the expected interval to 72h); an EMPTY SOURCE/COLLAPSE pair firing on H4's ordinary zero-email windows, since its metric is a delta against inventory it can't directly observe (fixed with an exemption + wider zero-streak window); and a daily EMPTY SOURCE alarm on H5's correctly-empty first run (fixed with the dated grace period above).

03Open questions, defects, and next moves

Items marked italic are Steve's inference from the data, not something explicitly logged.

Open questions

  • Does H4's card burst normalize into steady weekly output, or was it a one-time backlog drain? — worth another two or three weeks before concluding either way.
  • Is the H3 weak-signal cadence (each sector once per ~10 weeks) actually enough volume, or does the rotation need to be faster? — one thin run isn't enough evidence yet.
  • Should Simo's outreach identity send from @askelventures.com or @gmail.com? Raised by Varia with Micke back on 2026-04-07 — still open as of the 2026-08-28 autonomy-policy update, and it blocks full outreach maturity for H3.

Known defects

  • Estonia has no dedicated sourcing cron despite being in-thesis geography — coverage is ad hoc via H2 only.
  • firmakauppa.fi (H1) structurally can't surface EBITDA — every lead needs a human follow-up before it's card-worthy. Not new, not fixed this month.
  • H4 email alerts still can't deliver profit, age, or asking price — WAF still blocks listing detail pages, so leads are flagged for manual verification, never auto-scored pass/fail.
  • "Cron reports vanishing after long turns" — one of the five original silent failures — is logged in cron-sourcing-health's own notes as worked around, not fixed.

Potential improvements

  • Give H4 a way to reach listing detail pages (a non-datacenter egress path) so profit/age/asking-price can be scored automatically instead of flagged for manual review.
  • Stand up any Estonian source at all — even a manual monthly check beats the current zero coverage.
  • Once H3 has a few more rotations of data, revisit whether the sector order or cadence should change based on which sectors actually yield candidates.
  • Consider a lighter-weight "did this fire and was it non-empty" smoke check that runs sooner than daily, so a dead account (like H4's three-week gap) gets caught in hours, not weeks.
